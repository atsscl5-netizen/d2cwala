FAQ

Questions brands ask us

Something else on your mind? Email admin@d2cwala.com.

01Which brands do you work with?
Indian D2C brands that already sell online and want to grow across more channels. Beauty, personal care, fashion, food and wellness are a natural fit, but we're happy to talk to any category.
02Do I need to be on Shopify?
No. We can work with your current store. If you're planning a move to Shopify or a rebuild, we can handle that too.
03How much does it cost?
It depends on how many services and channels you need. After the free audit call, we send a written proposal with scope and fees, so you know the cost before anything starts.
04Can I start with just one service?
Yes. Many brands start with one channel, such as ads or a marketplace launch, and add more once they see how we work.
05Who owns the ad accounts and store?
You do. We work inside your accounts with access you grant, and you can remove that access at any time.
06How soon will we see results?
Fixes like tracking and page speed can show up within weeks. Marketplace launches and ad scaling usually take a few months to settle. We'll give you a realistic timeline in the plan.

Overview

What AI sales chat does for you

Understands real questions

Shoppers type the way they speak. The assistant works out what they actually need.

Sells, not just answers

It suggests products, compares options and adds items to the cart inside the chat.

Knows when to hand over

Tricky questions go to your team with the full conversation attached.

Recommendations

Suggestions that fit the shopper

It asks a follow-up question when it needs to, then suggests products that match the shopper's needs and are in stock.

  1. Reads your live catalogue and stock
  2. Explains why a product suits them
  3. Offers an alternative when something is sold out
RecommendationBuilt from the shopper's answers
  • Matches their need
  • Only in-stock products
  • Explains the reason
Checkout help

From question to cart in one place

Shoppers can add products, ask about delivery and returns, and move to checkout without hunting through pages.

  1. Adds products to the cart from the chat
  2. Answers delivery, return and payment questions
  3. Shares the right policy, not a generic link
In-chat checkout helpNo page hopping
  • Add to cart
  • Delivery questions
  • Return policy

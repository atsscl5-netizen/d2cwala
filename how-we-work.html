Example plans

What a first few months can look like

Two typical starting points. Your plan will depend on your brand, budget and channels.

Example plan

Launching a skincare brand on marketplaces

A brand selling only on its own site wants to reach shoppers on Nykaa and Blinkit.

  1. FirstChannel planPick the hero products and the right marketplace for each.
  2. NextAccounts and catalogueBrand registration, listings, images and A+ content.
  3. ThenGo live with adsSmall marketplace ad budgets on the hero products.
  4. After launchReview and expandKeep what sells, add quick commerce cities.
Why it works: launching a few strong products well beats listing the whole catalogue at once.
Example plan

Fixing a leaky Shopify store

Ads bring visitors, but too few of them buy.

  1. FirstFind the leakCheck tracking, speed and where shoppers drop off.
  2. NextQuick fixesCompress images, remove slow apps, fix broken tracking.
  3. ThenRework product pagesClearer benefits, shade guides, reviews near the button.
  4. After launchTest checkoutOne change at a time, measured against the old version.
Why it works: fixing the store first makes every rupee of ad spend go further.
